# Professional Case Studies — Energy, Engineering and Digital Transformation

These case studies summarize Donyada Weankhuntod's reported professional responsibilities. They deliberately omit employer-confidential metrics, systems, datasets and documents.

## 1. Energy Cost and Performance Analysis
**Context:** Industrial energy management at Covestro Thailand.  
**Scope:** Electricity, steam, fuel, and energy cost per unit of production.  
**Approach:** Analyze consumption and cost drivers, reconcile operational information, use normalized performance indicators and prepare insights for management.  
**Transferable capabilities:** Cost analysis, data quality, quantitative reasoning, reporting, and decision support.  
**Evidence to add:** Approved anonymized example, validated business impact, tools and exact reporting cadence.

## 2. ISO 50001:2011 → ISO 50001:2018
**Challenge:** Transition an established energy management system to the revised standard while meeting Thai requirements.  
**Contribution:** Led transition activities involving normalization, EnPI/EnB methodology, audit readiness, documentation and plant implementation.  
**Transferable capabilities:** Regulatory interpretation, systems thinking, governance and cross-functional rollout.  
**Evidence to add:** Non-confidential scope and independently verifiable results.

## 3. DEDE Digital Reporting / E-Service
**Challenge:** Translate Thai energy reporting obligations into a workable digital process.  
**Contribution:** Led an industrial-side e-service/e-form initiative, coordinating stakeholders and end-to-end implementation requirements.  
**Transferable capabilities:** Government interface, process redesign, stakeholder alignment, user adoption and digital transformation.  
**Boundary:** This is not a claim of employment by DEDE or ownership of the government platform.

## 4. Process Control and Global Knowledge Exchange
**Context:** Process Control Technology Engineer at Covestro.  
**Contribution:** Supported APC improvements after technical training in Germany; initiated cross-site knowledge exchange and contributed to alarm/HMI and global directive alignment with Thai requirements.  
**Transferable capabilities:** Operational engineering, international collaboration and standardization.

## 5. AI Academy and Digital Enablement
**Context:** Covestro Global AI Academy (2024–2025).  
**Contribution:** Completed global training and explored AI-enabled approaches for energy, ESG data and workflow improvements.  
**Boundary:** Training and applied exploration should not be presented as production AI deployment without separate evidence.

## Portfolio evidence checklist
- Problem and baseline
- Role and decision authority
- Tools and data sources that may be publicly disclosed
- Implementation steps
- Verified outcomes and measurements
- Lessons learned and sustainable handover

Never upload proprietary dashboards, raw plant data, customer data, credentials or internal corporate directives.
